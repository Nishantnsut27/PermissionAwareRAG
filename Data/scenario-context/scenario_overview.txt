SC-001 - Seller S001 Settlement Shortfall
==============================================================================

Seller           : S001
Primary date     : 2026-03-12
Window           : 5 March 2026 to 9 March 2026
System           : Settlement & Payout Service (settlement_ledger)
Owning dept      : Operations
Classification   : CONFIDENTIAL
People involved  : Rahul Sharma (Support), Vikram Singh (Engineering), Neha Gupta (Operations), Aditya Verma (Business)
Incident report  : INC-2026-0312
Support ticket   : TKT-2026-0312-114
Call transcript  : CALL-2026-0313-018
Relevant policy  : Payment Settlement Policy (POL-FIN-002)
Relevant runbook : Payment Recovery Runbook (OPS-RB-004)

ROOT CAUSE / BASIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The settlement ledger deduplication key was changed during the v1.8 to v1.9 schema migration and omitted the order identifier for reversal entries. When the batch was reprocessed after a transient job failure, each reversal was deducted twice, reducing the payout.

IMPACT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
S001 settlement short by INR 4,82,000; 1,914 reversal entries double-deducted; payout delayed by two days.

RESOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hotfix restored order_id plus entry_type to the deduplication key, the affected batch was reprocessed, and the shortfall of INR 4,82,000 was credited on 14 March 2026.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After a partition expansion, the order event consumer group entered a rebalance loop because the maximum poll interval was smaller than the time required to process a batch. Events were repeatedly reprocessed and never committed.

CROSS-SCENARIO RELATIONSHIPS
==============================================================================

Some scenarios are causally linked. These links are deliberate and
are consistent across all related documents.

SC-006 -> SC-012
  The size chart defect in SC-006 inflated the S004 return rate.
  That elevated rate, still present in the trailing 30-day average,
  triggered the automated account suspension recorded in SC-012.
  An Engineering defect produced an Operations risk determination
  with commercial consequences.

SC-001 -> project PRJ-S001-SRH
  The settlement shortfall in SC-001 was detected by the seller
  rather than by any platform control. That finding opened the
  settlement reconciliation hardening project, whose status report
  is a separate document in the projects folder.

SC-002 -> SC-008
  Both concern the S002 batch integration. SC-002 is a data
  correctness incident in the stock pipeline. SC-008 is a security
  incident concerning the SFTP credentials that deliver the same
  files. They are independent but share the same integration surface.

SC-003 -> SC-011
  Both are S002 throughput incidents. SC-011 is order event consumer
  lag; SC-003 is order API connection pool exhaustion. Both are
  addressed by the same programme, PRJ-S002-TR, and both share the
  finding that the platform lacked a mechanism to degrade
  gracefully under load.

SC-005 -> SC-009
  The June 2026 label failover failure in SC-005 was the event that
  caused milestone M3 in the SC-009 conditional tier upgrade to be
  validated with a live test, rather than closed on configuration
  evidence as it had been in October 2025.

SC-004 and SC-001 (both S001)
  Two separate financial-correctness incidents on the same account
  within one month. Both were detected by the seller's own
  reconciliation, which is the finding recorded in the S001 account
  overview and in the settlement reconciliation project.
